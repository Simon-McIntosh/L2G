ITER 15 MA H-mode Equilibrium — Real EQDSK Baseline

Source: ITER shot 135011/7, t≈400 s (15 MA flat-top) — real psi field and wall from EQDSK (equivalent IMAS import; MDSplus unavailable on this host). All subsequent studies perturb this real equilibrium rather than using synthetic fields. Key parameters: R_omp=8.196 m, Bp_omp=366 mT, Bt_omp=4.009 T, pitch=5.22°, OMP gap=198 mm.

Field-Line Pitch Angle Sensitivity

q_perp = q_par × sin(α) — pitch angle α is the primary FLT amplification lever. Key result: α ∝ Ip/|f_pol| (R-independent — both Bp and Bt scale as 1/R so their ratio cancels). Halving Ip (15→7.5 MA) halves q_perp. Plasma displacement changes OMP gap but NOT pitch angle. All physics via Ampere's law using real Ip and |f_pol_vacuum| from EQDSK.

SOL Heat Flux Profiles — λ_q and Model Sensitivity

All profiles use validated l2g.hlm.general functions (single_exponential_psol, double_exponential_psol). The dominant uncertainty is λ_q (SOL power decay length): 1 mm → 3 mm spans a factor ~5 in peak q_perp. The near-field double-exponential shoulder adds ~1.8× to the peak regardless of λ_q. Nominal: q_perp=170 MW/m² at dr_sep=2 mm; all λ_q values greatly exceed tile material limits at close approach — tiles must operate well into the SOL.

Plasma Displacement and Shape Sensitivity

Rigid plasma displacement: pitch angle is R-independent, so q_perp changes only ~1.5% per 120 mm. The dominant displacement risk is OMP gap (wall proximity), not heat flux amplification — gap = 0 at ΔR = +198 mm outboard. Shape study (minor radius scan, parametric D-shape on real wall): counter-intuitive — larger plasma (larger a) has slightly LOWER q_perp (q ∝ 1/R) but dramatically smaller gap. LCFS intersects wall at a ≈ 2.17 m.

Short-Wave and Long-Wave Tile Misalignment

SW misalignment critical step height: δ_crit = λ_q × tan(α) = 0.137 mm at nominal ITER. Any step > 0.1 mm saturates q_perp amplification to ~10× (entire λ_q exposed to leading edge). LW misalignment (δ_LW > 0 = exposure) is exponentially sensitive: even 1 mm LW amplitude doubles q_perp at dr_sep = 2 mm because exp(δ_LW/λ_q_near) ≫ 1. Both effects use validated l2g.hlm.general functions.

Monte Carlo FLT Penalty Analysis — Operational Uncertainty

3000-sample Monte Carlo around the real ITER nominal (shot 135011/7). Sampling: Ip~U[12,15] MA, λ_q~LogN(1.5mm,σ=0.25), dr_sep~N(2mm,±0.5mm), δ_SW~HN(σ=0.3mm), δ_LW~N(0,±0.5mm), P_sol~N(100,10) MW. FLT penalty = q_perp / q_perp_nominal. KEY RESULT: 58% of samples are LCFS-grazing — cot(5.22°)=10.95 means the SW shadow (δ_SW×cot(α)) exceeds the 2mm nominal gap for σ_SW≥0.18mm. Dominant sensitivity drivers: δ_SW (Spearman ρ=+0.61) and δ_LW (ρ=+0.58). Ip and plasma displacement are statistically insignificant (ρ≈0). Actionable finding: tile manufacturing tolerance must be <0.18mm step height to avoid LCFS grazing. FLT penalties are driven by mechanical tolerances, not plasma operating parameters.
